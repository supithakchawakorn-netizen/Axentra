# Roadmap — V1 Social Pivot

## Milestone 1 — Identity and media foundation

- Creator auth and profile setup.
- Upload + watch flows for video.
- Live room creation and viewing.
- Basic comments and discussion read/write policy.

## Milestone 2 — Community discovery

- Explore feed ranking for relevance and trust.
- Community/topic navigation and profile improvements.
- Better onboarding for joining and participating in communities.

## Milestone 3 — Reputation loops

- Reputation panel (insight, consistency, transparency, trust).
- Helpfulness feedback on video/live surfaces.
- Contribution milestone tracking and trust-aware ranking.

## Milestone 4 — Moderation and safety

- Community role model (owner/mod/member).
- Moderation actions and quality safeguards.
- Better abuse reporting and trust-signal protection.

## Milestone 5 — Retention and growth

- Return-session loops (continue watching, community streaks).
- Feed tuning and quality experiments.
- Creator/community growth surfaces based on contribution quality.

## Out of scope

- Brokerage integrations
- Trading/exchange flows
- Financial marketplaces
- Speculative economy mechanics
- Finance-specific AI summaries and market quote systems
