# Varg Packs — Project Overview

## What it is

Varg Packs is a web-first social platform for identity, trust, and contribution.
Video and live sessions are the primary media format, but the core product is
reputation and belonging inside communities.

## Why now

- Trust is degrading across large social platforms.
- Users want places where contribution quality matters more than vanity reach.
- Community-first products retain users through identity and progress, not hype.

## Audience

- **Community members**: people joining discussions, sharing useful content, and building reputation.
- **Creators and hosts**: people publishing videos and running live sessions.
- **Moderators and admins**: people protecting community quality and trust.

## V1 product shape

- Public home, explore feed, and live directory.
- Creator/community profile pages with reputation panels.
- Video watch and live room pages with feedback and discussion.
- Studio for upload, live, and settings.
- Community-first analytics and moderation-oriented trust signals.

## Product philosophy

- Build reputation, not followers.
- Optimize for trust depth, not scroll depth.
- Reward contribution quality and consistency.

## What it is not

Varg Packs is not a brokerage app, trading app, exchange product, or financial
marketplace. It does not execute trades and does not build speculative economy
loops.

## North-star metric (V1)

Weekly returning sessions on trusted creators and communities.
